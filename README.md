# 📊 [COOP Data Fellowship Projects]: [Presenting deliverables for group data analysis projects]

This repository displays the produced deliverables from projects I worked on as a data fellow for COOP, an organization based in the San Francisco Bay Area. 


As a fellow, I worked in a group of 4 to analyze data, gather insights, and present findings to an audience of 20+. Insights benefit food and safety, as well as digital marketing for larger organizations. 

    The Goal: Identify insights that will support stakeholder decision-making, and create actionable plans 

    The Impact: Identified at-risk areas for food and safety, top performers for advertising spending, and actionable strategies for all stakeholders.

⚙️ Tech Stack & Methods

Projects for COOP often involved SQL, Google Slides, Excel, Google Docs, and Tableau.

🗺️ Project Architecture

NYC Restaurant Violations Analysis: Worked with SQL + Excel to clean data, producing visualizations in Tableau, then combining all insights into a presentation-ready slide deck.

Digital Marketing Analysis: Worked with SQL + Excel to clean data, and calculated metrics such as CPC, CPA, CPvM, CVP, CTR, and Viewability. Introduce new metrics, including Valued Cost, which assesses how much spending results in Conversions.

📊 Key Insights & Outcomes

Instead of just saying "I did X," say "I found Y."

    Insight 1: I discovered that lower restaurants were associated with identified area codes.

    Insight 2: I found that device conversions were mainly concentrated on smartphone interfaces, and despite Sharethrough and PulsePoint having the highest conversion rates, Yieldmo and Xander generated the most productive value for companies' advertisements.

Keep this brief so users know where the "meat" of the project is.
